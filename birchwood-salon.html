Our Story

A little studio with a lot of heart

Birchwood Salon opened its doors in 2016 with a simple idea: create a calm, light-filled space where every guest feels genuinely cared for. No rush, no pressure — just skilled hands and honest advice.

Tucked into a quiet corner of Richmond, our boutique team keeps things small on purpose. That means more time with your stylist, a look built around you, and results you'll actually want to recreate at home.
