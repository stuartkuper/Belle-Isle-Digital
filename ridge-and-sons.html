About Ridge & Sons

Three Generations of Richmond Craftsmanship

Founded in 1999 by Bill Ridge, Ridge & Sons is now run alongside his sons Tom and Derek. We're a family business in the truest sense — and we treat every home like it's our own.

Licensed, Insured & BBB Accredited

We carry full contractor licensing for the Commonwealth of Virginia and maintain comprehensive liability and workers' comp insurance on every job — so you're protected.

Family-Owned, Locally Operated

When you call Ridge & Sons, you talk to a Ridge. No call centers, no runaround — just direct access to the people who'll show up at your house and do the work right.

10-Year Workmanship Warranty

We stand behind every nail we drive. Our 10-year workmanship warranty means that if something isn't right, we'll come back and make it right — at no cost to you.
